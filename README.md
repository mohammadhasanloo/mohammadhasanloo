## Mohammad GharehHasanloo

AI developer working on data-centric machine learning. Master's student and
research assistant in Information Systems and Technology at York University,
Toronto.

My research is on **data selection** and **data validation**: choosing which
training examples to keep for fine-tuning under a compute budget, and which
validators to run per record under a hard deadline. Both decisions turn on a
signal that is expensive or unknown at the point you need it. The approach is the
same in each case, which is to compute that signal exactly on a small anchor, fit
a cheap surrogate there, and decide from the surrogate rather than paying for the
full measurement.
